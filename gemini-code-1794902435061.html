01 / RESEARCH TOPIC

Should Elementary School Teachers Speak English Fluently?

Yes, elementary school teachers should speak English fluently for three main reasons:

1. Accurate Foundation

Children imitate their teachers easily.

Example: If a teacher pronounces daily instructions like "Line up, please" correctly, students will naturally build good pronunciation from a young age.

2. Global Learning Resources

Teachers can access international teaching materials.

Example: A teacher can use interactive science videos or games from foreign websites to make classes more engaging.

3. Habit Building

Daily exposure removes the fear of learning a foreign language.

Example: Greeting students in English every morning makes them treat English as a normal communication tool, not a scary exam subject.
02 / PERSONAL REFLECTION

Self-Reflection on Learning English

How well do you understand English?

I am at an intermediate level. I can understand daily conversations, main ideas in articles, and movies with English subtitles, though I am still improving my active speaking skills.

How do you learn English on a daily basis?

I integrate English into my daily routine by watching English content on YouTube, listening to podcasts or music, and setting my phone's language to English.

Which part of learning English do you like most? Why?

I like listening and vocabulary enrichment the most because I can learn naturally through movies and songs, allowing me to understand real-life expressions without feeling stressed.

What has made your learning more difficult?

My biggest challenges are overthinking grammar rules when speaking and the lack of an English-speaking environment for daily practice.

02B / CORE LEARNING STRATEGY & SUBTITLE STUDIO

Overcoming Challenges: Shadowing & Subtitle Strategy

What strategies have you tried to make your learning easier?

I practice the shadowing technique by repeating sentences from native speakers in videos, and I focus on delivering my message first rather than worrying about perfect grammar.

1
Watch with English Subtitles (CC)

Connect how words are written with how native speakers pronounce them in real context.

2
Shadowing (Repeat Immediately)

Imitate the intonation, rhythm, and pronunciation directly from the video subtitles.

3
Message Over Grammar Perfection

Build speaking confidence first without freezing over complex grammar rules.

Practice Line: "Line up, please! Let's learn together step by step." Open on YouTube
